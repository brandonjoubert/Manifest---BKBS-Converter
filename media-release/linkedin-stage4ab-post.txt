Stage 4a and 4b are live across all three editions — Python, PHP, and WordPress. The Claim Ledger now has a complete write and read path.

What Stage 4a achieves (claim writers + publication rules)

Before 4a, approving an entity only flipped a status column. Now approve writes actual claims — promoting pending claims to approved, inserting manual claims, superseding outdated values. Reject and needs_edit do the same. Only entities approved at least once make it into live exports. Pending-only and rejected entities are excluded.

Why this is essential

Without claim writers, the ledger was a read-side curiosity. Stage 4a makes the claim lifecycle complete. Every approve/reject decision produces auditable claim records instead of silent column overwrites. The diff between what a scan proposed and what is live becomes queryable.

What Stage 4b achieves (export adapters)

Every export format — llms.txt, graph.json, schema.org, agent.json, robots.txt — now reads from ResolvedEntity objects, not raw database rows. The adapters are identical across Python, PHP, and WordPress. The publish pipeline resolves latest approved claims, then passes results to format-specific adapters. Output is byte-compatible across all editions.

Why this matters for production

Exported files are always derived from the latest approved claims. A rescan proposing a new description will not change your live llms.txt until you approve that claim. Adding new export formats becomes implementing one function against a stable interface.

Bug fixes from full E2E testing

We ran end-to-end across all three editions. This surfaced real issues:

- "Edit before approve" showed for all statuses, not just pending. Fixed in all three editions.
- PHP crawler leaked <title> text into body descriptions. Fixed by stripping <title> before extraction.
- WordPress scan buttons were unlabeled with multiple sites. Now shows site name.
- PHP entities page was missing manual entry link. Added.

All verified: pytest 46/46, Stage 0–2 pass, Stage 4a tests 8/8, Stage 4b contract check pass.
