The web is changing again — and most business websites are not ready.

For 20 years, a “web presence” meant pages for people: marketing copy, menus, contact forms, SEO for Google.

That world is not ending. But it is no longer enough.

A new layer is forming on top of the open web:

- AI assistants answering on behalf of buyers
- Agents comparing suppliers, shortlisting vendors, and drafting recommendations
- Systems that don’t “browse like a human” — they look for structured, trustworthy facts

If your business only exists as HTML written for eyes, agents will scrape, guess, and often get you wrong.
Wrong services. Wrong regions. Wrong capabilities. Lost demand you never see.

This is the new web presence problem:
not “do we have a website?”
but “can machines understand us accurately?”

That’s why I built and open-sourced Manifest BKBS Converter.

It helps turn a traditional business website into a dual-purpose presence:

- Humans still get a normal, browsable site
- AI agents get a living knowledge base — services, capabilities, facilities, policies, evidence — that you can review and approve before it goes live

Output is practical and deployable today:
llms.txt, graph.json, schema.org JSON-LD, and related machine layers — published to your real domain.

In other words: your business becomes queryable, not just crawlable.

This is not a gimmick file and not “AI SEO magic.”
It is infrastructure for the agent era of the web — the same way mobile-responsive sites became non-negotiable last decade.

Open source. Apache 2.0. Free to use.
Works on your PC, Python hosts, and classic PHP shared hosting.

I’d love you to try it.
Clone the repo, point it at a real website (even your own), run a scan, review the entities, and publish. Tell me what works, what breaks, and what’s missing — early feedback from real businesses and operators matters more than polish right now.

Contributions are very welcome too.
Whether you’re a developer, designer, technical writer, or someone who simply found a bug on shared hosting: issues, pull requests, docs improvements, and test reports all help. If you’ve ever wanted to contribute to open source in the AI + web space, this is an open door.
